    Connection notes

    • Connect trainer works with any smart trainer that has ERG over Bluetooth. The app picks the protocol after connecting: the standard FTMS (Fitness Machine Service, used by almost every current trainer), Tacx FE-C over Bluetooth (older Tacx firmware) or the Wahoo trainer protocol (older Wahoo firmware). A power meter or a trainer without control connects read-only: you see your power, but ERG is not available. Updating the trainer firmware usually adds FTMS.
    • Heart rate comes from a separate Bluetooth strap (Connect HR) or, if your trainer relays one, from the trainer itself.
    • Close Zwift, MyWhoosh, the trainer maker's app and similar apps first. The trainer accepts only one Bluetooth controller at a time.
    • Windows, macOS, Android, ChromeOS: open in Chrome or Edge.
    • iPhone / iPad: Apple doesn't allow Web Bluetooth in Safari or Chrome. Open this page in the free Bluefy browser from the App Store.
    • Bluetooth only works on a secure page: https:// or http://localhost. To share the app, host this single file on GitHub Pages, Netlify Drop, Cloudflare Pages or any https web space and send the link.
    • ERG tips: start pedalling before the first target, and use the small chainring with a middle cog so low targets stay reachable. ERG takes a few seconds to settle after big steps.
